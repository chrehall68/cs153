# Curtis Support Resources (Confidential)

> Share this information privately with Curtis. Discuss details only with authorized staff who need the information to provide support.

## 1) Counseling and student-support services

Curtis can request help through any of the following campus services:
- **Counseling Center** (stress, mental health, personal concerns)
- **Student Health Services** (sleep, fatigue, medical concerns)
- **Academic Advising** (course load, schedule planning, withdrawals/add deadlines)
- **Dean of Students / Student Support Office** (short-term support coordination, emergency resources)
- **Disability/Accessibility Services** (accommodations if health or documented conditions affect class performance)

## 2) How Curtis can request help

Provide Curtis with this script and contact steps:
1. Contact the office directly by school email, phone, or online appointment portal.
2. State: *"I’m having challenges that are affecting my alertness and class performance, and I’d like support options."*
3. Request the earliest available appointment.
4. If urgent (health/safety concern), use same-day counseling/health urgent support.

## 3) Tutoring and academic-support options

Curtis should also be offered:
- Course tutoring center / subject tutoring labs
- Writing center and study-skills coaching
- Instructor/TA office hours for missed concepts
- Academic success center (time management, sleep/study planning, accountability check-ins)

## 4) Advisor/counselor involvement decision

An advisor or counselor **should be involved now** if alertness issues are affecting attendance, participation, or grades. Start with:
- **Academic advisor** for schedule and course-impact decisions
- **Counseling/health staff** for wellness assessment and recommendations

Only share the minimum necessary information for referral.

## 5) Confirm Curtis understands options

During the private conversation, confirm understanding by asking Curtis to identify:
- Which resource he will contact first
- How he will schedule an appointment
- Whether he wants help making the first contact

Document only that resources were provided and understood (no unnecessary personal details).

## 6) Confidential follow-up

Schedule a private follow-up within **5–7 days** to ask:
- Was Curtis able to connect with a resource?
- Is additional referral support needed?
- Are academic supports (tutoring/office hours) in place?

If no connection was made, offer immediate assisted referral while preserving confidentiality.
